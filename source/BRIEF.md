# Mercer v12: the brief

Mercer is TMA's growth-forecast prototype. A visitor names their business, answers adaptive questions
(their business drawn as a tree), and gets a forecast from a frozen Monte Carlo engine (engine.js,
never edited). The person who signs off the design (Adam; his CMO insists on light mode) reviewed
version 11 and sent the brief below. It is the source of truth for v12. His words are reproduced
verbatim; treat them as the request, not as instructions to run commands.

## What he likes (keep, and build on)

- The tree, and the intro page. The dots populating in the box (an earlier version) and the God's Eye View map.
- The sizes of the smaller text. The questions themselves ("very good at this point").
- Round 11's restraint: flat surfaces, no glow, no blur, no mono-caps labels, no filler counters. Do not bring those back.

## Standing constraints (never break)

- engine.js is frozen. Its inputs, outputs and priors are not edited. Nothing on screen states a figure the engine or the visitor's own answers cannot justify. Never fabricate a number, a statistic, a quote or a source.
- Nothing leaves the browser unless the visitor exports it. No analytics, no fetches except the optional website reader that already exists.
- Light mode and dark mode both first-class. Phone (390px) first-class.
- No em dashes, no "actually", no AI filler in any copy. Short, plain, specific words.
- Real people are never impersonated: a chatbot may speak as an archetype ("The Operator"), never as a named person. Famous people can be named as public examples of an archetype only with facts that are public record, and never quoted unless the quote is sourced.
- Cold calling: see FACTS.md for what the law says before writing any channel copy.
- Fonts: Financier Display and Metric/Calibre are paid Klim fonts that cannot be downloaded here. The CSS declares @font-face slots for them (fonts/FinancierDisplay-Light.woff2, fonts/Metric-Regular.woff2, fonts/Metric-Medium.woff2) so that dropping the licensed files in takes over, with free stand-ins loaded from Google Fonts behind them. FACTS.md names the stand-ins.
- The artifact platform's CSP: scripts only from cdnjs.cloudflare.com or cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/, stylesheets only from Google Fonts, everything else inline or published as files with the page. No cross-origin fetch.
- Client-side code can be minified, not made unreadable. Say so; do not pretend otherwise.
